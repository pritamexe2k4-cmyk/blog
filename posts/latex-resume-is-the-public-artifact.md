The resume lives at [pritamexe2k4-cmyk/resume](https://github.com/pritamexe2k4-cmyk/resume). LaTeX. Two versions: with photo and without. That is the public artifact for the job pipeline.

I am an AI/ML fresher in Hyderabad. The resume has to survive a PDF parser and a human in sixty seconds. A Google Doc named Untitled does neither.

## Why git

A resume that is not in version control gets overwritten in the wrong direction. Git gives me:

- A locked layout I can diff
- A public URL I can put on applications
- A place other agents in my stack can read without asking Notion

Notion still holds the tracker, the deadlines, the weekly report. The PDF that leaves the house comes from this repo.

## What this site is for

Logfile is not a second resume. It is the long form behind the bullets: RAG that refuses, agents behind one POST, dual-write rules, GATE and DSA notes when they are specific enough to publish.

If a bullet on the resume cannot be opened as a post or a repo, it is too soft. That is the edit rule.

## Honest status

The resume exists. The RAG design is locked and the code is not shipped. Agentic-ops is a README. Applications are the actual bottleneck. I will not use this blog to hide that.
